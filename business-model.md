# **Business Model Canvas: STARGATE**

**Product:** STARGATE (Review Gating & Reputation Micro-SaaS)

**Target Market:** Local SMBs in India

**Pricing:** ₹299 / month

## **1\. Front Stage (Customer-Facing)**

### **🎯 Customer Segments**

* **Primary (Buyers):** Independent local brick-and-mortar SMB owners (cafes, salons, dental clinics, and gyms) heavily dependent on Google Map Pack rankings for daily footfall.  
* **End-Users (Non-Paying):** Walk-in consumers who scan QR codes on counters/walls for instant digital incentives (freebies, discounts, digital guides).

### **💡 Value Propositions**

#### **The Core Problem**

Local businesses struggle to collect positive Google reviews from satisfied clients while preventing negative feedback from publicly damaging their ratings.

#### **The Solution (STARGATE MVP)**

An automated, 2-way review-gating engine:

1. **Scan QR:** Customer scans the custom counter poster/stand to claim a digital perk.  
2. **Rate Experience:** A lightweight mobile landing page prompts the customer to rate their visit from 1 to 5 stars.  
3. **Low Rating (1–3 Stars):** Routes the user to an anonymous feedback form. Submitting alerts the business owner instantly via Telegram bot while granting the user access to their freebie.  
4. **High Rating (4–5 Stars):** Prompts a clear CTA to leave a public Google Review. Clicking opens the official Google Review page and immediately unlocks the freebie link.  
5. **Printable QR Poster PDF:** The business receives a custom-branded, high-quality PDF poster to print and display at their reception or on walls.

### **📢 Channels**

* **Direct Walk-Ins:** In-person visits to local merchants during off-peak hours with a 30-second live mobile demo.  
* **WhatsApp Outreach:** Direct messaging for follow-ups, onboarding asset delivery, and monthly renewals.

### **🤝 Customer Relationships**

* **Personal Assistance (High-Touch Onboarding):** Face-to-face pitch, custom account creation, digital setup, and collecting ongoing merchant feedback.  
* **Automated Services (Hands-Off Maintenance):** Self-operating review routing, instant private Telegram alerts, and automated monthly ROI reports.

## **2\. Back Stage (Internal Infrastructure)**

### **⚙️ Key Activities**

* **Sales & Outreach:** Walking into local businesses, conducting live 30-second phone demos, and closing ₹299/month subscriptions.  
* **Instant Digital Onboarding:** Provisioning custom client URL slugs, creating/attaching freebie links, and generating high-resolution QR poster PDFs.  
* **Product & Infrastructure Maintenance:** Managing the Next.js web app, database, and Telegram Bot API engine for 99.9% uptime and zero-delay alerts.  
* **Retention & Account Management:** Delivering monthly WhatsApp ROI reports (*"You gained 14 Google reviews this month"*) and collecting monthly UPI payments.

### **🔑 Key Resources**

* **Intellectual / Digital:** Proprietary review-gating codebase, cloud infrastructure (Vercel & Supabase), digital freebie template library, and domain name.  
* **Sales Hardware:** Personal smartphone for live, in-person merchant demonstrations.  
* **Human Capital:** Solo founder handling software development, walk-in sales, and digital onboarding.  
* **Financial Infrastructure:** Merchant UPI account (Razorpay / PhonePe / Paytm) for collecting recurring payments.

### **🌐 Key Partnerships**

* **Cloud & Infrastructure Providers:** Vercel (web hosting), Supabase (database), Telegram Bot API (instant alert delivery), and Google (review destination).  
* **Payment Processors:** Razorpay / Paytm / PhonePe Merchant for processing ₹299 UPI transactions.  
* **Beta Merchant Advocates:** First 5–10 pilot business owners serving as local case studies and word-of-mouth referral sources.

## **3\. Financial Base (Bottom Line)**

### **💸 Cost Structure**

#### **Software Infrastructure (Near-Zero Fixed Overhead)**

* **Domain Name:** \~₹800 – ₹1,000 / year  
* **Hosting & Database:** Vercel & Supabase (Free Tier to start)  
* **Telegram Bot API:** Free (unlimited instant alerts)

#### **Transaction & Operational Fees**

* **Payments (manual UPI for now):** ₹0 fee — ₹299 received = ₹299 kept. *If* collection ever moves to a gateway (Razorpay / PhonePe / Paytm), ~2% applies (approx. ₹6 per ₹299 subscription)  
* **Sales & Conveyance:** Minor transit/fuel costs for local walk-in visits  
* **Demo Collateral:** Single laminated sample poster for in-person demos (\~₹20)

### **💰 Revenue Streams**

* **Primary Micro-SaaS Subscription:** **₹299 / month** per merchant for automated review gating, instant Telegram alerts, and digital freebie delivery.  
* **Upfront Cash Flow Option:** **₹2,999 / year** upfront (gives merchant 2 months free and secures upfront capital).  
* **Optional Add-On Revenue:** One-time **₹499 setup fee** for custom digital freebie design (e.g., custom Canva graphics or menu integration).
