# STARGATE — Sales + Demo Checklist (close paid from day one)

Target: independent cafes, salons, dental clinics, gyms. Price: **₹299/month**
(₹2,999/year). No free pilots — confidence close. Companion doc: `ONBOARDING.md`
(runs after the sale).

## 0. Walk-in kit (carry every time)

- Phone: charged, mobile data ON, `/s/test-cafe` funnel bookmarked
- One laminated demo poster (~₹20)
- Printed UPI QR for ₹299 collection
- This checklist memorized: 30-sec demo → objections → close → 3 details

## 1. Targeting

- Independent shops where the **owner is present**. Off-peak hours only
  (roughly 11am–12pm, 3–5pm) — never during lunch or evening rush.
- Skip chains and franchises (no decision power on the floor).

## 2. The 30-second demo (your phone, their eyes)

Opener: “Can I show you something in 30 seconds?” Then, narrating:

1. Open the demo funnel → tap **5 stars** → “Happy customers land straight
   on your Google review page — plus they get a gift, so they actually do it.”
2. Back → tap **2 stars** → type anything → submit → “Unhappy ones message
   YOU privately instead — and you get this alert instantly.” (Show the
   Telegram-style flow; gift still unlocks.)
3. Closer, one line: “Good reviews go public, bad reviews come to you.
   ₹299 a month, and your QR is live today.”

## 3. Objections (one line each)

- “No time.” → “Setup is entirely on me — 10 minutes of yours, total.”
- “We already have reviews.” → “This gets you far more of them — every
  happy customer gets asked, with a gift attached.”
- “₹299 is too much.” → “One extra customer covers the full year.”
- “Won't this invite complaints?” → “Complaints already exist — now they
  reach you first, privately, instead of sitting on Google.”

## 4. Close on the spot

- Collect **₹299 via UPI now**. No “I'll come back tomorrow.”
- Collect the 3 setup details: exact shop name, Google review link
  (Maps → business → Share), freebie URL + title.
- Promise: “Your QR page and poster arrive on WhatsApp today.”
- Then run `ONBOARDING.md` per shop, same day.

## 5. Follow-up loop (protects renewals)

- **Day 2 visit**: poster on the wall? First real scan done? Fix on the spot.
- **Day 30**: WhatsApp the ROI line from their dashboard
  (“14 review taps + 6 private feedbacks this month”) → collect renewal.
- Lapsed? One visit beats three messages.
